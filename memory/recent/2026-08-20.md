## Collective troubleshooting, member conflict calendars, and production mail (archive of August 19 work)

- Added collective team event types to Availability Troubleshooting in `d2fecc3` (`feat: troubleshoot collective team availability`). Troubleshooting now evaluates every active, enabled, non-excluded collective member's synced calendars, confirmed bookings, and personal working hours. It identifies the blocking member while redacting that member's calendar event, calendar, and guest details.
- Troubleshoot selection uses event-type IDs, avoiding collisions between personal and team slugs. Round-robin event types remain intentionally excluded with an explicit explanation because their "any member free" behavior needs a different diagnostic visualization.
- Corrected team event calendar filtering in `8784b0a` (`fix: scope team conflict calendars per member`). Every team member can open the team's Conflict calendars page, see only their own busy calendars, and save only their own selection. A non-empty selection restricts that member's conflicts for that event type; no selection means all of that member's busy calendars. One member's save cannot expose, replace, or suppress another member's choices. Creation also rejects calendar IDs not owned by the submitting member.
- Focused regressions proved that another collective member's conflict remains visible when the creator has selected calendars and that saving one member's selection leaves teammates' rows untouched. The targeted tests, formatting, and strict Rust lint pass were reported clean.
- Deployed the changes from clean `cascade-main` revision `aaacddf` as `1.15.1-cascade.13`, pinned to digest `sha256:d130baba6bb3d8c0bd65fc74a639d11cc31fd7ff23ae716688113a9669916bd0` through `~/Dev/cascade-calendar`. Gmail initially rewrote both From and Reply-To to `arthur@cascade.tax`; adding `calendar@cascade.tax` as an authorized Gmail Send As identity fixed the sender, and end-to-end mail was subsequently confirmed from `calendar@cascade.tax`.
